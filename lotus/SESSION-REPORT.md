# Lotus Proposal — Session Report

## What We Built
A single-page HTML branding & packaging proposal for the Lotus client, published live at `arcade.pietsuess.com/clients/lotus/`.

## What We Did

### Voice Document
- Researched Piet's writing across pietsuess.com, NAVIGATOR/TunnelRunner games, Tumblr, Salt Spring Centre of Yoga profile, IMDb, and project descriptions
- Created a permanent voice guide (`memory/piet-suess-voice.md`) documenting tone, vocabulary, sentence patterns, and anti-patterns
- This document is now available for all future copywriting work

### Proposal — Design
- Started from a pre-existing template with dark theme, Lato + Playfair Display fonts, scroll animations
- Bumped root font size from 16px to 19px
- Increased all small type sizes (labels, step numbers, bullets, terms, CTA)
- Made phase headings bold white full-width bands that break across the entire screen
- Added poetic layout to hero intro with four indent levels spanning the full viewport
- Styled three words differently: "colour" in yellow, "shape" in Rubik Mono One, "MOOD" in wide-spaced uppercase
- Moved pricing total below the breakdown (not above)
- Right-aligned the total against the midpoint

### Proposal — Copy
- Rewrote all copy from generic/corporate to Piet's voice
- Hero intro rewritten as a poem with Piet's exact words
- "Deliverables" → "What we'll make"
- "Terms" → "How it works"
- Step names rewritten: "Consultation" → "Getting to Know Lotus", "Brand System Development" → "Building the System", "Post-Delivery Support" → "I'm Still Here"
- All bullet points rewritten in conversational tone
- Closing headline: "This work is best when we're all having fun. Let's go."
- CTA: "Start →"

### Proposal — Pricing
- Started at $5,000, evaluated and raised to $7,500
- Final breakdown: Phase 1&2 combined at $4,200 / Phase 3 at $2,800 / Phase 4 at $500
- 25% deposit ($1,875)
- $150/hr additional revisions
- 3–5 weeks estimated timeline

### Structural Decisions
- Removed price from hero (only appears in Investment section)
- Removed per-phase timelines
- Combined Phase 1 & 2 into single price line
- Removed duplicate phase titles (white band handles it)
- Removed preamble divider (poem flows directly into phases)
- Removed contact info from hero (only in closing)
- Fixed mobile responsiveness on white band headers
