## Design Details
- **Fonts**: Lato (body/headings), Playfair Display (serif body/italic), Rubik Mono One (the word "shape")
- **Colours**: `--black: #0a0a0a`, `--white: #f5f2ee`, `--accent: #b8a898`, `--muted: #e8e0d4`
- **Special word styling in hero poem**:
  - "colour" — yellow (#FFCC00), bold
  - "shape" — Rubik Mono One, chunky flat display font
  - "MOOD" — wide letter-spacing (0.7em), uppercase bold Lato
- **Phase headings** — full-width white bands, dark text, bold uppercase
- **Noise texture overlay** — SVG fractal noise at 30% opacity, fixed position
- **Scroll animations** — IntersectionObserver fade-in on sections and staggered steps
- **Responsive** — single column below 820px, nav links hidden on mobile
