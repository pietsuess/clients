Unprofessional is a quarterly night of cabaret, where you never quite know what you'll get. Launched on Maya Suess's birthday in February 2022, the show lives in the back room of The Parkside Lounge on Houston and Attorney in the Lower East Side.

The programming is truly intergenerational, introducing younger performers to the legendary performers from the neighborhood. From Coney Island legends to drag kings, from performance artists to folk musicians, every edition is a different world.

"Allied Productions exemplifies one of the things I love about this neighborhood, which is the diverse legacy of experimental queer and radical art and performance. At the Unprofessional Variety Show, I try to make the programming truly intergenerational."
- Maya Suess, EV Grieve, Feb 2024

The Venue

The Parkside Lounge. A dive bar and performance venue catering to the artists, musicians, and freaks of the Lower East Side since 1908. 317 East Houston Street, New York, NY 10002.

Maya Suess

Artist, educator, and arts administrator. Born on a small island off western Canada's coast. BFA from Emily Carr, MFA from Simon Fraser. She makes drawings, installations, videos, performances, and other mischievous entities.

Klovis Gaynor

Musical accompaniment at nearly every edition. Queer musician and "baroque punk" artist. Queens-based. Sometimes joined by Mamie Minch on guitar.

The Deal

Sliding scale cover. All monies go directly to the performers. Tickets through Humanitix, where 100% of booking fees go to charity. That's the whole deal.
