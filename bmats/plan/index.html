Decisions made

  • Jul 6Kajabi stays as the store and email system. We build a custom watch experience around it instead of fighting its limits.
  • Jul 6Viewers log in with an emailed watch link plus a 6-digit code. No more passwords for watching the film.
  • Jul 6Video delivery moves from Vimeo to Cloudflare Stream: pay-per-view delivery with per-viewer protected links, instead of a forced Enterprise contract.
  • Jul 6The small Vimeo account stays alive only as the source of backup links for support.
  • Jul 6Gifting gets its own fixed-price offer. Kajabi cannot attach gifts to pay-what-you-want checkout, which is why gifting has been confusing.
  • Jul 6The main website gets rebuilt custom (off Squarespace) and the mailing list moves from Mailchimp into Kajabi, once the critical path is done.
  • Jul 7Planning number for August: 7,500 to 10,000 purchases, more if the paid ads land. Everything downstream (video delivery, support, emails) gets sized for that.
  • Jul 7Jeremy keeps editing the site himself, and the "upcoming screenings" spreadsheet that Lonnie fills stays. Whatever replaces Squarespace must keep both as easy as they are today.
  • Jul 8The new site is a faithful rebuild of the current look, consolidated from eleven pages to seven. Real fonts, real copy, every image self-hosted.
  • Jul 9Everything now lives on Cloudflare in the film's own account: the rebuilt site, the watch portal and the purchase database each have their own address there. Hosting cost stays $0 until event traffic warrants the $5 plan.

Subtitles on your TV: today vs after the move

The number-one support headache. Every path gets equal or better after the move; none gets worse.

Nothing
