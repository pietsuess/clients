Decisions made

  • Jul 6Kajabi stays as the store and email system. We build a custom watch experience around it instead of fighting its limits.
  • Jul 6Viewers will log in with an emailed watch link plus a 6-digit code. No more passwords for watching the film.
  • Jul 6Video delivery moves from Vimeo to Cloudflare Stream. Current usage (about 205 TB in the last year) is far past Vimeo's 2 TB monthly limit; Stream handles the same traffic for a few thousand dollars a year instead of an Enterprise contract, with stronger per-viewer link protection.
  • Jul 6The small Vimeo account stays alive only as the source of backup links for support.
  • Jul 6Gifting gets its own fixed-price offer. Kajabi cannot attach gifts to pay-what-you-want checkout, which is why gifting has been confusing.

4 · Support helper & ticket tracking

The chat helper solves the repetitive stuff automatically; everything else lands in one organized place instead of Jeremy's inbox.

  • Chat helper on every page, including the main Squarespace site: can't log in / no email / won't play / TV trouble / gift / payment
  • "Didn't get your watch link?" resolves itself: the helper re-sends it automatically
  • Helper collects email, device and an optional screenshot before a ticket is filed
  • Every ticket tagged by category, country, device and browser, automatically

  • Refund-reduction ladder built into the admin page: extend first, backup link second, refund last
  • Nightly copy of all tickets into a Google Sheet for easy browsing
