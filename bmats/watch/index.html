Watch on your TV

An HDMI cable from your computer to the TV is the most reliable way, every time. Any inexpensive cable works.

Subtitles

English subtitles are on from the start, in the film's own style. Use the CC button inside the player to pick another language. Your choice sticks for the whole film.

Full closed captions, with sound descriptions for viewers who are hard of hearing, are in the same menu under Canadian English.

Casting to a TV with Chromecast can drop subtitles on some models. If that happens, AirPlay or an HDMI cable carries them reliably.
