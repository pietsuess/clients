Watch on your TV

An HDMI cable from your computer to the TV is the most reliable way, every time. Any inexpensive cable works.

Subtitles

With subtitles Off the film plays as it did in theatres: the Nepali dialogue carries its English translations. The CC button in the player controls has full subtitles in eight languages, and your choice sticks for the whole film.

Closed captions, with sound descriptions for viewers who are hard of hearing, have their own section in that menu.

Casting to a TV with Chromecast can drop subtitles on some models. If that happens, AirPlay or an HDMI cable carries them reliably.
