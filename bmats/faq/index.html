Getting in

How do I get into the film?

When you rent, the film starts playing right on the rental page. A watch link also lands in your email a moment later. That link is your way back in: open it on any device and the film is there. Nothing to install, nothing to remember.

Is there a password?

No. There is no password and there never will be one. Your watch link is the only thing you need. If yours ever goes missing, write to Hello@MPWRCHANGE.COM and a fresh one will be sent.

My watch link has not arrived
  1. Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from Between the Mountain and the Sky.
  2. Try searching your inbox for mountain.
  3. Give it a few minutes. Email sometimes dawdles.

A resend-it-yourself option is coming soon. Until then, email Hello@MPWRCHANGE.COM and a real person will send your link over.

Can I open the link on a different device?

Yes. The same link works on your laptop, phone, tablet, wherever you like. Open the email on the device you want to watch on and follow the link from there.

Your rental

How long do I have, and how many viewings?

Seven days of access from the moment you pay, and unlimited viewings inside that week. Watch it alone, watch it again with someone, on any device. The event itself runs August 12 to 19.

How much does it cost?

You choose. It is pay-what-you-want, and $35 is the suggested amount. Pick a preset on the rental page or type your own.

Where does the money go?

40% of every rental goes directly to BlinkNow, supporting the children and community at the heart of the film. The rest keeps the film moving through the world.

Watching

Which devices can I watch on?

Laptop, phone, tablet, or your TV. Any modern browser works; Chrome and Firefox are the most dependable. For the TV, see Watch on your TV.

Which subtitle languages are there?

Eight languages: English (including full closed captions), Dutch, Italian, French, Hindi, Spanish, Latin American Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.

Pick your language right under the film, or use the CC button inside the player. Your choice sticks for the whole film.

Can I pause and come back later?

Yes. The film remembers where you stopped and offers to pick up there when you return on the same device. Pause anywhere, come back any time inside your seven days.

How do I make it full screen?

Press the full screen symbol at the bottom right of the player controls. On a phone, turning it sideways helps too. If full screen refuses to work, try Chrome or Firefox.

Watching on your TV

What are my options?

Three good ways:

  1. HDMI cable from any laptop to the TV. The most reliable way, every time, and any inexpensive cable from a hardware store works. There is no need for an expensive one.
  2. AirPlay from an iPhone, iPad, or Mac to an Apple TV or AirPlay-capable TV. Subtitles come along.
  3. Cast button to a Chromecast or Google TV. One caution: some Chromecast models drop subtitles. If subtitles matter to you, use HDMI or watch on the device directly.

AirPlay and Chromecast both need your phone or laptop and the TV on the same wifi network. That is the most common snag.

Full step-by-step instructions for each, ordered for your device, are on the Watch on your TV page.

Where are the AirPlay and cast buttons?

They live in the player controls, next to the volume and subtitle buttons. On a phone the control bar starts collapsed: tap the small arrow at its right end to reveal the rest.

On a phone, tap the small arrow at the right end of the player bar to reveal the rest of the controls: volume, CC for subtitles, settings, AirPlay or cast, and full screen.

What does the AirPlay menu look like?

After you tap the AirPlay symbol, a menu of nearby devices appears. Your TV shows up in that list when both are on the same wifi. Tap it and the film moves to the big screen.

Can I use my smart TV's built-in browser?

Honest answer: TV browsers are the least reliable way to watch, and we steer people away from them. AirPlay, the cast button, or an HDMI cable from a laptop will treat you far better.

Gifting

How do I give the film to someone?

Use the Gift a rental instead link on the rental page, right under the pay button. A gift is a fixed $35. You add their name and email, write an optional note, and can pick a send date up to six months out. On the day, they get a gift email with a watch link of their own.

Does a gift give me access too?

No. A gift gives access to the person you send it to, not to you. If you want to watch as well, there is an add a rental for yourself option at the gift checkout for $15 more.

Can I choose when the gift arrives?

Yes. Send it right away, or pick any date up to six months out. You get the receipt and a copy of the gift email either way, so you can always forward it yourself.

Trouble

It says the video cannot play due to privacy issues

That warning is almost always the browser blocking the player, not your account. First test: open the watch page in a private or incognito window. In Chrome that is the three dots at top right, then New Incognito window. In Safari it is the File menu, then New Private Window.

If it plays there, it is a cookie setting, easy fix:

  1. In Chrome: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies, and allow them.
  2. In Safari: Settings, then Privacy, and uncheck Prevent cross-site tracking.

Reload the watch page afterwards. If the warning is still there, email Hello@MPWRCHANGE.COM and we will send a backup link that plays directly.

The player will not load or the film stutters
  1. Refresh the page and press play again.
  2. Try a different browser. Chrome and Firefox are the most dependable.
  3. On wifi, try moving closer to the router.

Those three fixes solve most playback trouble. If none of them do, we will make sure you get to watch the film: email Hello@MPWRCHANGE.COM.
