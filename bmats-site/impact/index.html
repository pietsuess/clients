9 Ways to Use Between the Mountain and the Sky to Inspire Change

1) Host a special screening for your board, team, or organization to watch the film together and spark meaningful conversation.

2) Schedule a staff gathering or community event where your group can watch the film and discuss its themes of purpose, hope, and global impact.

3) Reach out to local high schools and universities about including the film in social studies, global citizenship, or service-learning curricula. A discussion guide will be available when the film is released for streaming.

4) Email your friends, family, and colleagues to recommend the film and share how it inspired you.

5) Post your reaction or favorite moment from the film on Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, or TikTok tagging @BetweenTheMountain. You can link to the trailer and help spread the message.

6) If you’re part of a book club, social group, or mom’s group, suggest watching the film together and holding a special conversation about what it means to build a life of service.

7) Recommend the film to the leader of your favorite nonprofit organization, especially those focused on education, women’s empowerment, or global service.

8) Planning a retreat, leadership summit, or nonprofit conference? Use the film as keynote content to ground your event in inspiration and impact.

9) Buy out a theater for a screening in your community during the film’s release window. It’s a powerful way to bring people together around purpose and action.
