Tiny Home

A handbuilt home on wheels. Cedar board-and-batten, a corrugated skirt, and a carved cedar door.

Scroll

Small footprint,
full craft.

All of the wood is reclaimed, salvaged from a historic Salt Spring building. Built on a tandem-axle trailer, the home wears a board-and-batten exterior above a galvanized corrugated skirt, under a single-pitch shed roof with a deep overhang on the door side.
