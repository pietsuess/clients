01 · THE PROBLEM

Dedicated Inventories Are So 19th Century

Inventory data is the key input to forest operations, conservation, wildfire risk, and carbon MRV. Foresters are still collecting it manually, with tools & techniques from the 19th Century. It’s slow, frustrating, and usually measures <1% of what’s actually there.

Bullfinch fully automates the forest inventory process – no training, no stopping, no dedicated trip. Just do your job and the data flows. Continuously. Every time you’re in the field. For any reason.

MANUAL PLOTS · SMALL SAMPLES · <1% MEASURED

02 · HOW IT WORKS

Is It Really This Easy?

Wear. Bullfinch attaches securely to your vest and stays blissfully out of your way.
