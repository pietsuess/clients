Dedicated inventories are so 19th century.

Inventory data is the key input to forest operations, conservation, wildfire risk, and carbon MRV. Foresters are still collecting it manually, with tools and techniques from the 19th century. It's slow, frustrating, and usually measures less than 1% of what's actually there.

Bullfinch fully automates the forest inventory process. No training, no stopping, no dedicated trip. Just do your job and the data flows. Continuously. Every time you're in the field. For any reason.
