01 · CANOPY

The forest data stack still starts with a tape measure.

Canopy. Satellites, drones, and LiDAR model the forest from above, but they still need field data to know what is actually there.

Ground. Today's foundation is sparse manual plots: people walking transects, measuring trees, and entering observations by hand.

Bullfinch. Wearable sensors turn field work already happening below the canopy into continuous, plant-level ecological data.

SATELLITES · LIDAR · MODELS · STILL NEED GROUND TRUTH

02 · SENSOR LAYER

Wearable sensing for the people already in the forest.

Capture. Sensors collect below-canopy observations while foresters and land teams do their regular work.

Classify. Edge AI resolves those observations into structured signals for species, inventory, biodiversity, and forest condition.

Connect. The output becomes a ground-truth layer for registries, insurers, certifiers, land managers, and financial institutions.

WEARABLE SENSORS · EDGE AI · PLANT-LEVEL RESOLUTION
