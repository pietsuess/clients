02 · SENSOR LAYER

We capture data while your team is already in the forest.

Small. Instead of a massive LiDAR backpack, this is literally two phones in a small carrying case that is worn on the front of a surveyors kit.

Wear. The sensors move with the person already walking real terrain. Lightweight and simple.

Capture. Below-canopy signals are collected at plant-level resolution during normal field activity.

Resolve. Edge AI turns those signals into structured observations for species, inventory, biodiversity, and forest condition.
