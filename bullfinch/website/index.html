01 · PROBLEM

Forest inventory is still built on tape measures and small samples.

Field reality. Professional foresters still measure trees by hand across small plots, using the same core workflow that has shaped inventory for generations.

Data gap. Timber, wildfire insurance, carbon, biodiversity, and ESG decisions all depend on ground truth that is still scarce, expensive, and inconsistent.

Market pressure. Nature markets are asking for trust, but the reliable under-canopy data layer has not existed at operational scale.

MANUAL PLOTS · SMALL SAMPLES · HIGH-VALUE DECISIONS

02 · PRODUCT

Strap on. Walk. Data flows.

Zero training. Bullfinch uses phone-scale, off-the-shelf sensors plus proprietary edge AI and sensor fusion.

Passive capture. As a forester moves, the wearable collects species classification, DBH, timber volume, stocking density, and forest condition signals.

No extra survey day. The product turns normal field activity into repeatable inventory data without a LiDAR backpack or workflow reset.

Data product. Observations can feed maps, tables, reports, models, and partner systems that need trustworthy forest-floor inputs.

SPECIES · DBH · VOLUME · STOCKING DENSITY

03 · DATA LAYER

Bullfinch is the ground-truth layer for forestry, finance, and carbon.

For foresters. Denser operational inventory without adding more field burden to an already shrinking workforce.

For markets. Insurers, banks, carbon registries, and ESG providers need reliable forest-floor data to price risk and trust claims.

For platforms. Bullfinch creates a repeatable data stream for MRV, wildfire risk, biodiversity, restoration, and stewardship products.
