Bullfinch Earth / Client Brief

Bullfinch Earth launch system.

Project brief and production investment for the public website, field film, wearable data animation, brand kit, and investor-ready launch materials.

The product being described is the Bullfinch field intelligence system: wearable sensing that turns real forest work into continuous plant-level ecological data.

01 / What This Phase Does

Turn the pitch into a working public system.

The current build establishes the feeling: dark field space, red convergence, data moving through the forest, and a serious investor-facing cadence. This phase keeps that language and gives Bullfinch the actual market surface around it.

Website

Public launch site

A refined site architecture that carries the pitch flow into a clearer product, investor, pilot, and team story.

Film

Wearable field film

Short field-led video showing the wearable in context, with motion graphics explaining the data layer it creates.

Brand Kit

System for consistency

Logo, type, colour, motion, image, layout, and copy rules so the site and collateral feel like one company.

Sales Tools

Materials that close

Investor and pilot materials that make the ask clear, make the benefits legible, and reduce custom explanation.

Motion

Responsive

Launch
