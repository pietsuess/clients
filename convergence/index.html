Convergence 2026

A parking lot take-over · Sept 19 & 20 · Design walkthrough

This design is a hot, vibrant, layer upon layer of translucent risograph color. This poster lets the yellow glow like backlight through paper, while magenta and pink wash over one another, slightly out of register. I wanted the color layers and type hierarchy to do the work, and be bold while drawing the viewer in closer towards the subtle detail. I've split the two days, stacked top and bottom around the central wordmark, making a clear delineation of dates.

A closer look

The artistry, up close

Textures and layers, giving the whole piece an organic, analog feel.

Atmosphere

Dates in a softer key

The day headers are painted in a gentle, luminous tone, a quiet layer that lets the lineup take the lead.

Light

Light from within

The yellow carries the whole image like a lamp behind paper. Every color laid over it picks up that inner glow.

Heat

Headliners in full heat

The artist names rise to the front in hot magenta on electric yellow, the first note the eye catches.

Anchor

The returning wordmark

Carried forward and set at the heart of the poster, the teal letters drift gently out of register, ink resting on ink.

Motion

Layers in motion

The streaked, slightly out-of-register layers carry a real sense of movement, echoing the festival itself: separate elements drifting together and building energy across the weekend.

Craft

The maker's hand

Translucent passes settle over one another and the partner marks nestle softly in, the warm grain of true risograph.

Made by hand

Textures, Smudges, Distortion, deliberate choices, and the warmth of a design built by a person. This design feels like it was photocopied, smudged, and streaked, and layered on sheets, by a human hand.
