It is an improv show. We show up, we pick a theme, we make the rest of it up on the spot. There is no script, there are no second drafts, and there is definitely no plan.

Every show gets a brand new poster, a brand new idea, and a cast of people willing to make fools of themselves in public. Some nights it is peppers. Some nights it is a sasquatch. Some nights it is cheese doctors. You never quite know what you are going to get, but you are always going to get something.

We have been doing this for a while now, and we do not plan on stopping.

The Show

Long-form improv in the round. Doors at 7:30, show at 8. Themes change every time. Cast rotates. Nothing is rehearsed and that is the point.

The Group

A set group that performs together. The lineup evolves over time, but admittance is strict. This is not an open stage.

The Posters

Every show gets its own poster. They are half of the reason we keep doing this. All the art on this page is original, and it is all made for nothing.
