The design
A coastal cutthroat trout, drawn from life on a creek behind the shop. Cutthroats are the island fish you do not get on the first cast. The wordmark sits underneath in the house serif, slightly arched, embroidered in matching thread.
The hat
  • 100% softwashed chino twill, low-profile, unstructured crown
  • Self-fabric strap, antiqued brass slide
  • One size, adjustable, fits 56 to 60 cm
  • Bone is a warm putty cream. Sage is a faded coastal green.
Made & shipped
Embroidered in small runs in Vancouver. Designed on Salt Spring. We ship flat in a recycled mailer, normally same week. Island pickup is free at the shop.
Care
Wear it. When it needs it, spot clean with a cloth. Cold hand wash, air dry on a beer can to hold the brim. Never the dryer.

[ The Mark ]

One fish, drawn properly.

The cutthroat is a careful animal. We wanted a mark that earned its place on the front of a hat without trying to. The drawing is by hand, in two greens and a sand belly, with a faint red slash at the gill that you only see up close. We did not stylise it. The fish does that on its own.
