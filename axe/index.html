As children we roam free.

Our quiet thesis
A note from the shop Exploring is a basic part of human nature. It gives us peace, bonds us with our surroundings, and rewards us with a sense of accomplishment.

As children we roam free. Our untethered imagination transforms our backyard into a forest, a riverbed into the edge of the known world, the toolshed into a place where genuine adventure begins. A stick becomes a paddle. A walk to the bottom of the field is an expedition. We come home dirty and certain that something has been learned, though we could not yet say what.

Axe & Reel is built for the part of you that still recognises that feeling. We carry the gear of people who have lived outside, well, and are passing it on. Lightly used camping and fishing gear, vintage lanterns, 35mm cameras, packs that have already been somewhere. Boots, pants, socks, the right work shirt. A little inventory of the things that make leaving the porch easier.

Most of what we sell comes through consignment, so the shop is never the same twice. You wander in, you look, and now and then something on the bench answers a question you did not know you were asking. That, more or less, is the whole point.

[ 04 / Consignment ]

If you have something worth passing on.

60 / 40 in your favour
i.

Drop in for a look

Bring two or three pieces by the shop, Tuesday through Thursday. We talk it through with you on the spot. No appointment, no paperwork until we both know it is a fit.

ii.

We price it together

We have a sense of what moves on the island. You set the floor, we agree on the asking price, and we tag it. If it has not moved after sixty days, we mark it down before sending it home.

iii.

You are paid monthly

Sixty per cent to you, forty to us. We e-transfer on the first of every month with a one-page breakdown of what sold and what is still out. Simple, traceable, no chasing.
